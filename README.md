# Abstract2Title

Generate and evaluate publication-ready article titles from your abstract, with scoring, strengths, weaknesses, and top recommendations.

Abstract2Title is a standalone browser-based academic title assistant. It analyzes an abstract and returns exactly three title recommendations using different academic title strategies.

## Core workflow

Abstract
→ Concept extraction
→ Contribution identification
→ Title strategy generation
→ Title generation
→ Title scoring
→ Top 3 recommendation

## What the app returns

For each of the three suggested titles, Abstract2Title provides:

- Title Fit Score /100
- Title strategy
- Abstract alignment
- Clarity
- Specificity
- Academic tone
- Searchability
- Concision
- Strengths
- Weaknesses / improvement points
- Copy-title action

The highest-scoring option is marked as the Recommended Title.

## Three title strategies

1. Descriptive & precise
2. Contribution-oriented
3. Concise academic

## Privacy

The current version runs locally in the browser and does not send the abstract to an external AI service.

## Important note

Title Fit is a heuristic decision-support score, not a journal acceptance probability. Final title selection should consider the full manuscript, target journal conventions, study design, and author judgment.
